Why This Weekend Exists

We're fluent in almost everything — except the relationships that matter most.

You can run a home, a career, a full life, and still feel unseen by the person across the table. This isn't a fix-it workshop. It's a chance to slow down long enough to feel what's actually there — and to remember how to open.

A Retreat & A Festival

Come to transform. Stay to play.

There's real depth here — breath, honesty, the tender things we usually avoid. But this was never meant to be a weekend of unearthing heavy stuff and crying in a circle. It's a celebration, too: between the deep moments there's a sauna to melt into, a bonfire under the stars, long meals, music, and dancing in the barn till late.

The Arc of the Day

Five movements, from opening to celebration.

The heart of the retreat is one full day of transformation — paced to move between depth and delight, never all heavy, never all light.

01

Opening Circle

We gather as one group to build trust and name the patterns we all share — what relationship really is, and the kind of partnership we're all quietly reaching for.

02

Understanding Each Other

Men and women move into separate spaces to learn each other's inner worlds with curiosity and care — the needs, rhythms, and longings we so often misread.

03

Breathwork Journey

A guided holotropic breathwork session to move beneath the thinking mind, surface what the body has been holding, and find the root of the pattern.

04

Reconnection

Gentle inner work to come home to yourself — feeling held, supported, and whole. Not seeking. Full.

05

True Partnership & Celebration

We anchor a felt experience of true partnership, then let it all move — music, dance, and a proper celebration of the reunion of masculine and feminine.

Your Weekend

Three days on the land.

Friday
Arrival

Gather & settle

Arrive in the afternoon or evening, find your bed or your tent, and ease in over tea and light snacks. Nothing is asked of you tonight but presence.

Saturday
Transformation

The full day

The five movements, a grounding brunch and a dinner together, little delights between sessions — and as night falls, the sauna, a bonfire, and dancing.

Sunday
Integration

A gentle close

A light breakfast, an unhurried morning, and space to integrate what surfaced before you carry it home with you.
